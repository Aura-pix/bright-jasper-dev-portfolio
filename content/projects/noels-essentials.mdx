## The problem

A client needed an online store fast, with orders arriving where they already do business.

## What I built

A single page store with a product list and a cart. Placing an order goes through WhatsApp. I built it and handed it over within 24 hours.

## Stack

Next.js and JavaScript.

## Decisions and fixes

**One page.** A single page store keeps the build small enough to ship in a day and easy for the client and customers to use.

**WhatsApp for orders.** Order placement goes through WhatsApp.

## Status

Delivered to the client.
